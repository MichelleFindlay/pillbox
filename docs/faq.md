# Frequently asked questions

## Is Pillbox medical advice?

No. It helps you keep a record and reminds you of things worth asking about. Always follow your prescriber's instructions. In an emergency, call 999; for urgent help, NHS 111.

## I didn't get a notification

Check **Account → Notifications** is on for this device, and that your phone allows notifications from Pillbox (or the browser). On iPhone, Pillbox must be on your home screen. Notifications are sent by the scheduled tasks, so ask your administrator if none arrive at all.

## I took a dose but forgot to log it

Use **At…** next to the dose on Today to log it with the time you took it. Yesterday's doses are listed separately so you can catch up.

## My stock count is wrong

On the Meds page, open **Prescription collected** and choose **Set the exact number I have**.

## I changed my dose. Will my history change?

No. Changes only apply from now on, and each one is recorded in the medicine's history.

## Who can see my information?

You, and anyone you choose to share with, for the parts you choose. Private medicines and private pages are never shared. See [Sharing](sharing.md) and [Privacy and security](privacy.md).

## I've forgotten the PIN on a private page

The only way back in is **Delete everything** on that page and start again. That's what keeps it private.

## Can I move my data to another Pillbox?

Yes: **Account → Your data → Download all my data**, then **Import data** on the new one.

## Why does the Pregnancy page not appear?

If your body inventory is filled in and doesn't include a uterus, Pregnancy isn't offered. You can change the inventory any time in your health profile.
