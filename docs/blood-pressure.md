# Blood pressure

For high blood pressure. Your home target is shown at the top: below 135/85 (or 145/85 from age 80), unless your clinician has set a different one under **Your monitor and target**.

## Home monitoring

The way NICE recommends checking your blood pressure at home:

1. Press **Start a week of home monitoring**.
2. Sit quietly for 5 minutes, then take a reading. Press **1-minute timer**, then take a second.
3. Enter both (and your pulse), morning and evening, for 4 to 7 days.

The grid shows which mornings and evenings you've done. Day 1 is left out, and the rest are averaged and compared with your target. Share the result with your GP or practice nurse.

Tick **Irregular heartbeat symbol** if your monitor shows one. If it keeps happening, tell your GP.

## Readings

Morning and evening averages over 90 days, irregular heartbeat flags, and a chart with changes to your blood pressure medicines marked.

## Importing from your monitor

**Import from your monitor (CSV)** reads the file your monitor's app exports (such as Omron Connect). Importing again never duplicates. Withings monitors can connect directly in Readings.

## Medicines and side effects

Your blood pressure medicines are recognised by type. **Note a side effect** records common ones (a dry cough with ACE inhibitors, ankle swelling with amlodipine, dizziness). **Sick-day rules** appear only if you tick that your clinician has given them.

## Safety

- **180/120 or more**: measure again after 5 minutes. With chest pain, a severe headache, vision changes, confusion, weakness or breathlessness, call 999; otherwise contact your GP today or NHS 111.
- **Low** (under 90) with dizziness or fainting: sit or lie down, and contact your GP if it keeps happening.

## Checks

A yearly review (kidney function, potassium, urine ACR), and a reminder for blood tests 1 to 2 weeks after starting or increasing an ACE inhibitor or ARB.

**Clinician summary** prints your averages, chart, medicines, side effects and kidney results.
