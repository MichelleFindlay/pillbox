# Thyroid

For an underactive thyroid treated with levothyroxine (sometimes liothyronine).

- **Your thyroid medicine**: one-tap set-ups for morning (empty stomach, 30 to 60 minutes before breakfast) or bedtime dosing. Missed a dose? Take it when you remember unless the next is nearly due; never take two together.
- **Spacing**: Pillbox checks all your medicines and warns if calcium, iron, antacids or multivitamins with minerals are within 4 hours of your thyroid medicine (on the Meds page too).
- **Brand**: record it each time you collect; changes are noted in the medicine's history.
- **Blood tests**: latest TSH and free T4, and when the next TSH test is due (about 3 months after a dose change until settled, then yearly). Log results in [Blood tests](blood-tests.md): dose changes are marked on the chart. Stop biotin (in some hair and nail vitamins) for 2 days before a test.
- **This week**: a weekly check of symptoms that can mean too little or too much thyroid hormone, charted against dose changes
- **Good to know**: free prescriptions with a medical exemption certificate (MedEx) in England, and when to ask about your dose (pregnancy, starting oestrogen, low mood)
- **For your phone's medical ID**: a line to copy

**Clinician summary** prints your dose and brand, doses taken, dose history, TSH chart, symptoms and other medicines that affect absorption.
