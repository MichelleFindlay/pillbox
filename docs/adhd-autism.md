# ADHD and autism

No diagnosis is needed: use whatever fits.

## About you

For ADHD, autism, dyslexia, dyspraxia, tics or other: self-identified, waiting for an assessment, or diagnosed.

## Communication passport

How to talk to you: how you communicate best, time to process, sensory needs, touch, what helps if you're overwhelmed or in shutdown, what helps at appointments, what not to do, and someone who can help.

- **Show it**: full screen in large text, to hand your phone to someone
- **Print** it, and it's printed on appointment sheets too
- **Copy for an ICE / medical ID**, so it's there if you can't speak

**Reasonable Adjustment Flag**: you can ask your GP practice to add one to your NHS record, so services know the adjustments you need. Note when you asked.

## Waiting lists

Each assessment with the provider, route (NHS, Right to Choose in England, or private) and referral date: Pillbox shows how long you've waited.

## Assessment prep

Jot down examples as you think of them (childhood and school, daily life, work, relationships, senses, strengths) and print them for the day.

## Energy and senses (optional)

Energy (spoons), sensory load, meltdowns or shutdowns with what might have contributed, sleep, and burnout signs. If a week looks hard going, Pillbox gently says so. With cycle tracking, energy before a period is compared with other days.

## Medicines

ADHD stimulants can be marked as **controlled drugs** (prescription countdowns) and logged with **How it's working** (when it kicked in and wore off). See [Medicines](medicines.md). Checks remind you about blood pressure and pulse, weight, the yearly review and shared care.
