# Mind

An optional space for mental health. Switch it on or off in your health profile.

## Help now

The **Help** button in the top bar is always one tap away and is never locked. It lists UK crisis lines (Samaritans on 116 123, NHS 111 option 2, and others you can edit), your own contacts, and your safety plan. If you're in danger now, call 999.

## What's in Mind

- **Check-ins**: mood, energy, motivation and anxiety, with tags, triggers and notes, and trends over time
- **Safety plan**: warning signs, coping steps, people and places, and who to call (the NHS / Stanley-Brown format). Best written on a calmer day.
- **Coping toolkit**: what helps you, plus box breathing and 5-4-3-2-1 grounding
- **Questionnaires**: PHQ-9 and GAD-7, with scores over time
- **Journal** with prompts, and CBT thought records
- **Therapy and care**, mental health medicine notes, symptoms, self-care, activities, habits, wins, connections, a staying-well plan and an advance statement
- **Summary** to print for appointments

## Privacy

Mind can have its own **PIN** (unlock with a passkey: fingerprint, face or device PIN). Crisis contacts are never locked. Nothing from Mind is included in email summaries, and it's only shared with people you choose to share it with.
